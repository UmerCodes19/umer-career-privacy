Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Umer Career Intelligence ("the App") handles information when you use it to review professional profile information and career-related materials. The App is developed and operated by its individual developer ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information the App may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, the App may process:

  • Professional profile information, such as your name, headline, About section, work experience, education, skills, certifications, and public profile details.
  • Content and files you choose to provide, such as a résumé, portfolio links, project descriptions, screenshots, and exported profile data.
  • Information made available through LinkedIn when you authorise a supported LinkedIn connection and the relevant permissions have been granted.
  • Prompts, review notes, and generated feedback created while using the App.
  • Basic technical information necessary to operate and troubleshoot the App, if applicable.

The App does not intentionally request your LinkedIn password. You should sign in only through LinkedIn's own authorisation flow when one is provided.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and improve the App's career-related features, including to:

  • organise professional information and career materials;
  • analyse profile completeness, clarity, consistency, and presentation;
  • generate suggestions for improving profile content and professional materials;
  • produce summaries, reports, and drafts for you to review; and
  • maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

The App is intended to assist the user. It does not publish profile changes, send messages, or perform other LinkedIn actions unless a feature explicitly offers that action and you initiate or approve it.

3. Storage and processing

The App is designed for user-directed processing. Information may be stored locally on the device or computer where the App is run. If you choose to use an online hosting, database, or AI service, the information you submit may also be processed by that service to provide the requested feature.

You are responsible for reviewing the settings and privacy terms of any third-party service you connect or choose to use. Do not submit information that you do not want processed by those services.

4. Third-party services

The App may work with third-party services selected or configured by the user, such as LinkedIn, AI model providers, hosting providers, or developer tools. Those services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent privacy practices.

The App does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

5. LinkedIn information

If the App connects to LinkedIn, access is limited to the information and actions permitted by the LinkedIn products, permissions, and authorisations available to the App at that time. The App does not claim access to all LinkedIn account data.

LinkedIn is a separate service operated by LinkedIn. Your use of LinkedIn remains subject to LinkedIn's Privacy Policy and its applicable terms. You can manage or revoke an app's access through your LinkedIn account settings, where available.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required to address security, legal, or operational needs. Locally stored files can generally be deleted by the user from the device or storage location where they were saved.

If information is held by a third-party service, deletion may need to be requested from that provider and is subject to its policies and processes. To ask about information handled by the App, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your devices and accounts protected, do not share access tokens or credentials, and avoid placing secrets in project files or public repositories.

8. Your choices

You can choose what information to provide and which optional integrations or AI services to use. You may stop using the App at any time. You may also request clarification, correction, or deletion of information handled directly by the App by contacting us.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for professional and career-related use and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
